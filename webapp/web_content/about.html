About

Shop A Source lets you search for a product in plain English and compares prices for it across dozens of online stores in one place. Describe what you're looking for - "cheap waterproof hiking boots from target", "wallet under $20" - and an LLM works out the shops, sorting, and filters for you, so there's nothing to configure by hand.

You can choose which AI model does the parsing - Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI/Codex), Gemini (Google), or DeepSeek - from the picker next to the search bar, based on whichever the server has configured.
